# Code of conduct

## Our commitment

Miso welcomes contributors and users of all backgrounds and experience levels. Participate respectfully and focus criticism on ideas, code, and behavior rather than personal identity.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, patient, and constructive.
- Respect differing experiences, access needs, and technical opinions.
- Accept correction, take responsibility for mistakes, and help keep discussion useful.
- Protect other people's privacy. Never publish credentials, private messages, or identifying information without permission.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, doxxing, impersonation, spam, deliberate disruption, and repeated personal attacks are not acceptable. Retaliation against someone who reports a concern is also unacceptable.

## Scope and reporting

This policy applies to this repository, issues, pull requests, discussions, and project representation. For routine project moderation, open a minimal, neutral issue or discussion without sensitive personal details and request maintainer attention. For private reports of GitHub-hosted harassment, abuse, or threats, use the **Report abuse** feature on the relevant content/user or [GitHub Support](https://support.github.com/contact). GitHub's safety team can receive private reports; do not expose a reporter's identity in a public thread. Security vulnerabilities have a separate private reporting channel in SECURITY.md.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit/remove content, lock discussions, reject contributions, or restrict/block participation. They consider severity, repetition, impact, and willingness to correct behavior. A warning may be appropriate for a first minor incident; serious threats or privacy violations can result in an immediate ban. If you dispute a moderation decision, make a calm request for another review without repeating harmful or private content. Decisions remain subject to GitHub's own rules and safety processes.

Maintainers are responsible for applying this policy fairly. No promise of absolute confidentiality can override platform/legal requirements; avoid sharing unnecessary personal data.
