# Maintainers and governance

- Repository: Relay-harness/miso
- Maintainer and release reviewer: [@cicada0007](https://github.com/cicada0007)

This is a small maintainer-led community project. Issues propose changes; focused pull requests implement them. The maintainer decides scope, reviews permission/security/dependency changes, and approves releases. A substantial new data-access or upload feature needs an issue and explicit design before implementation.

No paid support or response-time guarantee is offered. Additional maintainers can be appointed by the current maintainer after sustained constructive contribution. Contributions are accepted under MIT, with third-party notices retained; no CLA is currently required.
